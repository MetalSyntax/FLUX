FLUX — Personal Reading App

Your privacy-first, offline-capable reader for manga, comics, and e-books. Native support for EPUB, PDF, CBR, and CBZ formats directly in your browser.

Features | Discover Free Books | Privacy Policy

Key Features

  • Privacy First: All your files are stored locally in IndexedDB. No accounts, no cloud servers, no tracking.
  • Formats: Read EPUB novels, PDF documents, and CBR/CBZ comic files with advanced zoom and panning.
  • Discover: Browse and download free classics from Project Gutenberg directly into your browser.
  • Statistics: Track your reading streaks, daily goals, and completed books.
